# MK discovery output

Use a compact list or table:

| Case | Recorded date | Lane | Recorded outcome | Reviewed law | Reader link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supplied case identity | Supplied date, with anomaly noted | Supplied lane | Supplied category/label | Returned citations | Supplied user-openable URL |

Omit fields not supplied rather than filling them from memory. Keep meaningful integrity/coverage notes alongside affected rows.

Do not translate an outcome category into a complete order. For example, a partial-grant category does not identify which provisions were granted, their effective dates, or the precise constitutional conditions. Those conclusions need judgment text beyond this discovery contract.

Similarly, `has_dissent=true` supports only the recorded flag. A judge filter may identify a case through a curated roster match; it does not prove that the named judge authored a dissent or specific reasoning.

If a recorded date is future-dated or inconsistent with the returned case identity, identify it as unverified metadata. Do not invent a corrected date. Offer the supplied reader link for source inspection and a report only if the user asks.

For a topic search, state the filters and that the results are a bounded discovery list. Do not promise exhaustive cases, holdings or treatment history.
